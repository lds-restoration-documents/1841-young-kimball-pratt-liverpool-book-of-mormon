**Calibration:** to test whether the proofreaders were rubber-stamping (most batches finished in 20–40 seconds), six errors were planted by the main session in pages of batches 10–15 and the originals kept in `work/canaries.json`: a dropped word (289), a changed letter (377 `unto`->`unte`), `;`->`:` (434), the last 8 words of the page removed (465), a name letter (584 `Jesus`->`Jesua`), and an inserted word (513 `he also had been stoned`). The proofreaders caught and correctly fixed 5 of 6; **the inserted word on 513 was missed** (log said no changes). All six planted errors were restored and verified against the originals. Reading: single dropped or altered items are mostly caught (about 5 in 6, a small test), an inserted extra word is the weakest case; and 0 real errors found in 49 random pages means the remaining page-level error rate is low but not zero (rule of thumb: at most about 6% of pages, i.e. up to a few dozen of the ~634 text pages, with some correction for the missed-error rate). The two real errors found in the whole book so far (a dropped last line on 326, caught by the OCR diff; a punctuation mark on 648) both came from checks other than the random sample.
