# 1841-young-kimball-pratt-liverpool-book-of-mormon
Markdown &amp; Plain Text extractions of the content of the 1841 edition of the Book of Mormon published by Brigham Young, Heber C. Kimball, and Parley P. Pratt in Liverpool, England. Reprint of the 1837 edition with British spellings. Extracted using AI.

Future editions of The Book of Mormon printed by the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints descended from this 1841 British edition, not the 1840 edition.
