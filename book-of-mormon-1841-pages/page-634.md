## CHAPTER VIII.

An epistle of my father Mormon, written to me Moroni; and it was written unto me soon after my calling to the ministry. And on this wise did he write unto me, saying, my beloved son Moroni, <!-- uncertain: a stray mark after son, not text --> I rejoice exceedingly <!-- uncertain: a stray mark before exceedingly --> that your Lord Jesus Christ <!-- uncertain: a stray mark between Jesus and Christ --> hath been mindful of you, and hath called you to his ministry, and to his holy work. I am mindful of you always in my prayers, continually praying unto God the Father in the name of his holy child, Jesus, that he, through his infinite goodness and grace, will keep you through the endurance of faith on his name to the end.
